Learn how to create and configure new prediction markets on True Market.


## Market Creation Guide

Creating a prediction market on True Market allows you to set up forecasting events and let the community trade on outcomes.

## Prerequisites

- Connected wallet with sufficient MOCA tokens for gas fees and liquidity
- Market description and clear outcome definitions
- Initial liquidity for market bootstrap (recommended: 10+ MOCA tokens)

## Creating a Market

**Step 1: Start Market Creation**
Navigate to the "Create Market" section and click "New Market"

**Step 2: Define Your Market**
- **Market Title:** Clear, descriptive title (e.g., "Will Bitcoin exceed $100K by end of 2025?")
- **Description:** Detailed explanation of the market question
- **Category:** Select relevant category (Crypto, Weather, Sports, AI, etc.)

**Step 3: Define Outcomes**
Create binary (Yes/No) or multi-outcome markets:
- **Binary Markets:** YES and NO outcomes
- **Multi-Outcome:** Up to 8 possible outcomes with custom labels

**Step 4: Set Resolution Date**
Choose when the market will resolve. Markets cannot be extended after resolution.

**Step 5: Add Liquidity**
Provide initial liquidity (minimum: 5 MOCA tokens) to bootstrap the market. You'll receive liquidity provider tokens.

**Step 6: Review & Deploy**
Review your market details and click "Deploy Market"

**Step 7: Confirm Transaction**
Your wallet will prompt you to confirm. Review gas fees and approve.

**Step 8: Wait for Confirmation**
Once confirmed on MOCA chain, your market is live!

## Market Configuration Options

### Resolution Method
- **Oracle Resolution** - Resolved by selected oracle providers
- **Community Vote** - Resolved through decentralized voting
- **Manual Settlement** - Resolved by market creator (trusted markets only)

### Market Type
- **Binary** - Two outcomes (most common)
- **Categorical** - Multiple outcomes
- **Scalar** - Numerical range outcomes

### Fees
Set creator fee (0-5%) earned when market trades. Platform takes 2%.

## Market Lifecycle

1. **Trading** - Users buy and sell shares
2. **Resolution** - Market outcome is determined
3. **Settlement** - Winning shares redeemed for $1
4. **Completion** - Market archived

## Gas Fees

Gas fees depend on MOCA network congestion:
- **Market Creation:** ~1-3 MOCA
- **Add Liquidity:** ~0.5-1.5 MOCA
- **Trade:** ~0.1-0.5 MOCA per transaction
